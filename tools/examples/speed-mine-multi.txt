# then which ones are air at six times (in order, one "Test passed/failed" per block; a seventh is a control nobody

# Expected with max-blocks=6: A and B at ~167, C and D ~170 later, E and F ~170 after that. max-blocks=4: the 6th click
# replaces the 4th, so D and E stay. max-blocks=2: only A and F break.
# (tp facing turns the feet to the point, so each aim is the block's top centre minus the eye height.)
